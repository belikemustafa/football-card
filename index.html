📖 Pitch Clash XI — Official Rules

1. 100 Footballers & Balanced 4-4-2 Squads

Draft from 100 modern superstars & all-time legends. Each manager gets 2 ATT, 4 MID, 4 DEF, and 1 GK balanced within ≤ 0.5 OVR. Outfield cards have 6 stats (SPD, DRI, CTL, FIN, PWR, DEF); GKs have 3 (DIV, HND, SAV).

2. 90-Minute Match Clock (5' Per Move) & Golden Goal

Each completed duel advances the broadcast clock by +5 minutes (18 moves = 90' Full Time). It is turn-based—never a real-time ticking timer! If tied at 90', Golden Goal (Sudden Death) begins and the next goal wins!

3. Distance-Scaled Goal Probability (Closer = Much Higher!)

  • Zone 4 (Inside Box): Close-range proximity bonus! Equal stats ≈ 64%, +10 edge ≈ 82%, defenders up close still ≈ 44% (capped 22%–92%).
  • Zone 3 (Edge of Box): Needs PWR≥SAV+5, FIN≥DIV+5, PWR≥HND+4 for 32%–56% (max 62%); drops to 10%–26% if missed.
  • Zone 2 (Midfield): Needs PWR≥SAV+9, FIN≥DIV+9, PWR≥HND+8 for 18%–38% (max 46%); drops to 5%–14% if missed.
  • Zone 1 (Own Half): Needs PWR≥SAV+13, FIN≥DIV+13, PWR≥HND+11 for 8%–22% (max 28%); drops to 2%–6% if missed.

4. ✨ Skill Moves, ⚡ Boosters, Stamina & Lockouts

  • ✨ Skill Move (+5 DRI): Once per outfield player per match! Boosts DRI by +5, triggers a flashy skill callout, and wins ties in dribbling duels.
  • ⚡ Stat Boosters (0–10): Win the ball = +1 Booster. Spend any number (+2 to active stats each).
  • 🔋 Stamina: Standard duel -16%, Long Shot -22%, resting recovers +18%. Tiers: 70–100% (0), 45–69% (-2), 25–44% (-5), <25% (-8).
  • 🔒 Defeated Lockout: Defeated outfielders are locked for 1 turn (GK exempt; automatic softlock safeguard).
