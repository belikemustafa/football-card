📖 Pitch Clash XI — Official Rules

1. Balanced 4-4-2 Squads & 6 Outfield Stats

Both managers receive 11 unique world-class cards: 2 ATT, 4 MID, 4 DEF, and 1 GK, automatically balanced within ≤ 0.6 OVR. Outfield cards have 6 stats (SPD, DRI, CTL, FIN, PWR, DEF); Goalkeepers have 3 stats (DIV, HND, SAV). First to 3 Goals (or 5 Goals) wins!

2. Pitch Zones & "Anyone Can Score"

Any outfield card (ATT, MID, or DEF) can carry the ball, advance, pass to a teammate in Zones 2–4, and score!

  • Zone 1 — High Press: Carrier SPD vs Opp ATT DEF (Opp picks from 2 ATTs).
  • Zone 2 — Midfield: Carrier DRI vs Opp MID DEF (Opp picks from 4 MIDs).
  • Zone 3 — Edge of Box: Carrier CTL vs Opp DEF DEF (Opp picks from 4 DEFs).
  • Zone 4 — Close to Goal: Carrier FIN + PWR vs GK DIV + SAV (Tiebreaker: Carrier CTL > GK HND).

3. Long-Distance Shots (From Zone 1, 2, or 3!)

At any zone before the box, fire a ⚡ LONG SHOT directly at the Opponent GK! It scores ONLY if ALL THREE conditions are met (with Stamina & Boosters applied):

  • Carrier PWR ≥ GK SAV + 5
  • Carrier FIN ≥ GK DIV + 5
  • Carrier PWR ≥ GK HND + 5

Long Shots cost -28% stamina. If saved, the opponent wins the ball (+1 Stat Booster) and your shooter is locked next turn!

4. Stat Boosters (Stack up to 10), Stamina & Lockouts

  • ⚡ Stat Boosters: Every time you win the ball (tackle, intercept, or GK save), earn +1 Booster (stack up to 10). Each Booster spent adds +2 to all stats for that duel (up to +20!).
  • 🔋 Live Stamina: Starts at 100%. Standard duel costs -22%, Long Shot costs -28%, resting recovers +15% per duel! Penalties: 70–100% (0), 45–69% (-3), 20–44% (-6), <20% (-10).
  • 🔒 Defeated Lockout: Any outfield card that loses a duel is LOCKED for 1 turn (GK is exempt; oldest locked card auto-unlocks if an entire line is locked).
